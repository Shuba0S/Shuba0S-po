📖 Project Overview

The Blood Bank Management System is designed to simplify the process of donating and requesting blood. It connects donors, patients, and administrators through a unified platform. With secure authentication, transparent tracking, and a clean UI, it ensures blood availability is managed efficiently and responsibly.

  • 🩸 Donors can register, donate, and view history
  • 🏥 Patients can request specific blood groups
  • 🛠️ Admins oversee requests, approvals & inventory
  • 📊 Real-time tracking of donations and requests
